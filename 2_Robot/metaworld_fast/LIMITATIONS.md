# Interpretation and numerical limitations

The synthetic ideal-point oracle responds to the original q coordinates. It does not see end-effector positions, task success or trajectory features. Given identical statistical random streams, enabling simulator realization must not change queries, responses, posterior summaries or EIG curves. Meta-World adds a realization audit, not an independent robotics-derived preference-learning gain.

The recorded motions are short, 50-action-step open-loop clips. This experiment learns no RL policy and does not compare RL returns. Finite simulator states and bounded submitted actions do not establish collision freedom, real-robot safety, task success or validity under arbitrary physical constraints. Environment action steps and MuJoCo physics substeps are separate counts.

Command encoding can be full rank and reconstruct q accurately while the executed motion remains weakly sensitive or saturated along some directions. Command reconstruction does not establish human perceptual identifiability or the geometry of a trajectory-feature preference model. Actual position-series separation, workspace saturation and motion-Jacobian singular values must be examined. No trajectory-feature utility is substituted to conceal a weak physical mapping.

Five interactions are a fixed computational budget, not guaranteed statistical power. The 128 rounds within each interaction are dependent; they are not 128 independent replicates. Finite T=128 results do not prove an asymptotic limit, convergence rate or theorem assumptions in every history. Between-interaction standard errors in tables are descriptive. No seed is dropped because its ranking is unfavorable. A mean based on fewer than five finite values is explicitly incomplete.

The beta sweep is correctly specified, with oracle and inference beta equal, and uses d=4. It does not assess beta misspecification and does not reproduce the manuscript's d=8 beta sweep. SG's original schedule stays at stage 0 with scale 1 and its saved initial center for all 128 actual questions. A lack of SG collapse over this interval is compatible with the prescribed schedule.

Posteriors, moments and EIG are numerical approximations. Marginal remeshing and finite-particle resample-move SMC preserve an intended likelihood target but do not eliminate integration error. A larger reference calculation does not retroactively repair the original 4096-particle interaction. Frozen-history checks and bounded refinement may remain unresolved. Independent reference Monte Carlo uncertainty is separately reported; two independent replays provide only an imprecise estimate of it, and the original interaction's own Monte Carlo uncertainty is not measured by the reference SE. A check reported as within reference uncertainty is not exact posterior certification.

EIG ratios compare against a finite seven-scale line family, not a globally optimal query. Off-grid methods can legitimately exceed one. Tiny unresolved denominators or integration discrepancies are recorded, not replaced with a floor or hidden by clipping. Risk is the trace of the entire covariance; realized squared parameter error is a separate outcome.

The named source manuscript was absent from the project at initial inspection. The explicit mathematical definitions in the supplied execution specification are implemented; an independent manuscript-to-code audit is not claimed. Any reuse requires source paths, hashes, actual seeds and reused ranges. No compatible saved statistical prefix was identified initially.

Detached execution and checkpoints protect against an SSH disconnect and support resuming the statistical workload. They cannot guarantee survival of a host shutdown, exhausted storage or unrecoverable numerical/environment failure. `STATUS.md`, process logs and per-candidate status records distinguish pending, executed, cache-hit and failed work. Only actual artifacts support a completion claim.
